One ranked relief plan from conflicting reports.

POSEIDON is a human-supervised command centre that verifies, ranks, allocates and re-routes disaster relief. Every piece of evidence carries a reliability score from 0 to 1.

Six steps, one loop, one human gate.

VerifyTier every report, merge duplicates, flag conflicts
PrioritizeRank areas by severity, vulnerability, urgency, confidence
AllocateMatch teams, boats, ambulances and supplies
RouteShortest safe path on a graph with closed roads removed
OperatorApprove, edit or reject. Nothing is auto-sent
Monitor, replanA break in the plan restarts the loop

Language models read and explain. Deterministic maths decides, so every score can be audited and replayed.
